Status: done — ZTronScrollView tested inside lucaszischka/BottomSheet (rev 3d1238d) on 27.0 and 17.5, preferred + `--forced`, ours vs SwiftUI `ScrollView` vs the package's own scroll wrapper. Report: `docs/design/BOTTOMSHEET-spike.md` (table, reading, recommendation).
Headline: **without `enableContentDrag()` the view is a drop-in for `ScrollView` in BottomSheet (identical on both OSes; the header/indicator moves the sheet).** With `enableContentDrag()` content and sheet compete and the winner flips with the OS, for ours and for the native view alike (27.0: ours scrolls, sheet frozen / native: sheet moves, no scroll; 17.5: the opposite). Disabling our view does not hand the drag to the sheet on 27.0 (pan stays attached), so the "apple scroll behaviour" recipe `enableContentDrag(!isTop)` + `.ztronScrollDisabled(!isTop)` works on 17.5 (identical to native) and on 27.0 only with the pan attached as `.high`/`.gesture` (DEBUG `-ZTronPanGestureMode`; default is `.simultaneous`). A sheet detent change shifts our offset by half the height change (S7 visible centre, native keeps it); `defaultScrollAnchor(.top, for: .sizeChanges)` makes it 0. SwiftUI `.scrollDisabled` and the twin give identical rows (DBG-scrolldisabled fix holds in a third-party sheet).
