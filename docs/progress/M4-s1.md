Decisions:
- Default range is 1...1, so `setZoomScale(2)` now clamps to 1 (UIKit behaviour); the two named tests plus TransformTests.resizePreserves set a 1...4 range. Zoom-enabled alignment is .center, so TransformTests' zoomed u now includes the 32 pt pad. Demo ProofScreen needs a range for its Zoom button (M2 proof stays 6/6).
- Coordinator method is `handlePinch(_:)` not `handle(_:)`: PanEvent/PinchEvent share `.cancelled`/`.frame`, so an overload is ambiguous at call sites.
- Geometry insets are zero (M5 reads content insets); fit/fill also use zero insets.
- Programmatic animated zoom: spring over progress 0..1; scale and the viewport point of the anchor are interpolated, so zoom(to:) has no first-frame jump and ends exactly on the solver result.
- willBeginZooming/didEndZooming only fire when the scale actually changed (a centroid-only pinch fires neither). A pan or other catch during a bounce ends zooming with didEndZooming at the presentation scale.
- Combined release: pan `.release` becomes didEndDragging(false) + endTracking while pinching or bouncing.
- Cold flags isZooming/isZoomBouncing live on the `.phase` channel; min/max are plain stored values (not observed); the design said `.metrics` channel.
