D30 (the finding): on the fallback path (iOS 15 / `onGeometryChange` unavailable or forced) `ContentSizeKey` and `ViewportMetricsKey` preferences propagate to every ancestor, so an inner `ZTronScrollView` overwrote the outer's content size/viewport (outer `ocw=300` instead of 900 → fit → never scrolled, even under `.independent`). First matrix: 17.5 and 27.0 forced 6/12 red, preferred green. Now each scroll view clears both keys for its ancestors. Red→green with a unit test on the forced path. Real iOS 15/16 nesting was therefore broken before this.
